## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is lemlist?
lemlist is an AI-powered sales engagement platform that finds leads, personalizes outreach, and runs multichannel campaigns (email, LinkedIn, calls, WhatsApp, SMS) - all in one place. From lead generation to enrichment, outreach automation, and deliverability, everything works together, so you don’t need multiple tools.
### Who is lemlist for?
lemlist is built for sales and growth teams who want to spend less time on manual work and more time closing deals.
It helps:
→ Sales leaders ramp teams faster and hit targets
→ Sales ops streamline workflows and scale efficiently
→ SDRs and AEs focus on conversations instead of admin
Whether you're a solo founder, agency, or scaling team, lemlist gives you everything you need to build high-performing motion.
### How does lemlist help with email deliverability?
Every lemlist seat includes built-in deliverability tools, including lemwarm, our automated warm-up and deliverability booster. It helps you build sender reputation, avoid spam, and keep your emails landing where they should, outside of spam.
### Does lemlist comply with SOC 2 security standards?
Yes, lemlist is SOC 2 certified. This certification ensures that our systems and processes adhere to rigorous controls to protect your data and maintain high security and reliability standards.
### Can I integrate lemlist with my current sales stack?
Yes. lemlist connects seamlessly with tools like HubSpot and Salesforce, so you can even manage lemlist outreach without leaving your CRM. With native integrations, MCP, and API access, it fits right into your team's existing workflow.
### How can I get started with lemlist and see how it works?
You can book a personalized demo with our team or explore lemlist on your own with a 14-day free trial. Once you sign up, the dashboard will guide you through the setup step by step, and if you need help, our support team is just a click away via the in-app chat.
