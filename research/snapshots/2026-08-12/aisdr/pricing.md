## Frequently Asked Questions
Find the answers here to your most pressing questions
### Why quarterly commitment instead of monthly?
Good outbound takes time to set up properly and produce results. Quarterly gives enough runway to warm up sending infrastructure, test messaging, and ramp up performance. A full PoC on whether AiSDR will work for you is something a 30-day window rarely allows, as warmup alone takes 30 days for maxing deliverability. If you’d rather test the waters first and see what our AI is capable of, our Solo plan runs month-to-month.
### What happens if I don’t use all my messages in a quarter?
Unused messages roll over into the next quarter so long as your subscription is active. If you don’t renew your subscription, unused messages will expire when your subscription ends.
### Can I roll over unused messages to the next billing cycle?
Yes, so long as your subscription is active.
### How does your pricing compare to competitors?
Most of our competitors offer quarterly or annual contracts, with only a few offering low-budget trial options. Quarterly pricing allows you to try out AI for outreach, prove the concept for your business situation, and either continue or cancel your subscription. With competitors, you’re locked in until the end of the year.
### How long does it take to set up AiSDR?
Setting up AiSDR takes less than 1 business day as most of the steps are handled by Ami AI, our AI Strategist. In case you need a larger number of domains and mailboxes, the infrastructure setup might take ~3 days.
### What if I need to scale mid-quarter?
You can upgrade your plan mid-quarter if you need a larger contacts volume. We’ll prorate the difference so you only pay for the additional capacity you need.
### Do you offer annual plans with additional discounts?
Yes, annual plans are available for teams that want long-term cost savings and stability. Annual plans give a 20% discount compared to quarterly plans.
### How is AiSDR different from Salesloft/Outreach/Instantly?
Those tools help you send messages. AiSDR helps you decide _what to send_ and _who to send it to_. AiSDR combines strategy, research, infrastructure, and execution, allowing you to measure success by meetings booked.
### Can I pause outbound?
Yes, you can pause outbound at any time. This is useful if you’re changing ICPs, updating messaging, or shifting focus. AiSDR also works for inbound messages, so you can continue engaging inbound leads while reworking your outbound.
### Do you integrate with HubSpot?
Yes, AiSDR has a native, two-way HubSpot integration and works closely with several agencies and partners who rely on HubSpot as their CRM. You can sync lists, personalize with HubSpot properties, log activity, create and save call scripts, track intent, and even score accounts.
### What are AI-researched contacts?
AI-researched contacts are the net-new leads AiSDR finds, qualifies, and enriches for you each month based on your ICP and targeting criteria. The AI scans a much larger pool of raw data to surface them, so think of your plan’s number as leads actually added to your campaigns.
### What kind of results can I expect with AI outreach?
As a rule of thumb, AiSDR shows 1-3% conversion rate from a cold prospect to a booked meeting. These estimates are based on historical performance of teams using AiSDR, and vary based on the market fit, type of the offer and its strength. So on average, you can expect 1-3 meetings for each 100 contacts. They’re directional benchmarks designed to help you understand relative plan capacity.
### Can I send more messages than my plan includes?
To send more messages than your current plan supports, you’ll need to upgrade your subscription. To keep you from accidentally going over the limit, we set volume caps and notify you when you’re nearing your max. You can upgrade your plan inside the platform at any point.
### Can I have a free trial?
We currently don’t offer a free trial, but we’ve made our pricing as flexible and accommodating as possible. If you’re not getting results, you can cancel your subscription anytime.
### Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can terminate the subscription at your convenience for any reason. This gives you ultimate flexibility for finding the best sales software for you.
### Do I need an account with Sales Navigator to generate new leads for my campaign?
Nope, we got you covered. For prospecting, you don’t need a separate account, and you also don’t pay extra for lead generation as it’s already included in your subscription. So you can easily pull the leads you need to fuel your campaigns.
You only need a Sales Navigator account if you want to send LinkedIn InMail messages in your outreach sequence.
### When do I get billed?
You’re billed monthly starting from the day you sign up, for the duration of your contract (quarterly or annual).
### How can I make sure I don’t go over my monthly contact limit?
You’ll be able to monitor how many contacts you reach out to out via your Campaign Dashboard. Once you reach the limit, you’ll receive an in-app notification. At that time, you can choose to either pause your campaigns once you hit your limit, or you can choose to pay for any overage so that you don’t lose traction.
### Can you automate LinkedIn messages the same way as emails?
Sure! AiSDR can drop LinkedIn InMails, connection requests to prospects, and then send messages with multimedia to those who accept it.
### What if I don’t have a domain for outreach?
No worries! We’ll get one and set it up for you.
We also warm all your connected inboxes up in the background, fresh or seasoned, so you won’t ever have to worry about email deliverability.
### Does AiSDR integrate with other applications and CRM?
Yes, currently AiSDR supports integrations with Gmail, Outlook, HubSpot, Salesforce, Aircall, and Calendly.
### Do I need to create prompts and train the AI by myself?
Not at all. Our GTM Engineers work on prompting the AI in the background, while Ami AI is trained to generate GTM strategies, find leads, and build your sales personas that contain all the information your AI SDR needs to know about who it’s speaking with, how it should be talking, and what it should be talking about.
All you need is to have a conversation with Ami AI. No coding or prompt engineering needed 🙂
### How can I test the functionality of the product?
There are several options. **(A)** You can book a demo where our salesperson will set up a test campaign and you can see what it’s like to converse with our AI SDR. **(B)** You can start a conversation with our AI on your own. **(C)** You can try our AI Strategist, which will send 5 GTM plays based on your website to your inbox. **(D)** You can try AiSDR’s Solo plan and get a first-hand self-service experience at a smaller budget.
