## Frequently Asked Questions
Find the answers here to your most pressing questions
### What is AiSDR?
AiSDR is an AI-powered sales development representative that fills your sales pipeline with qualified meetings that show up. It combines signal-based targeting, the most precise list building in the market, GTM campaign idea generation, and omnichannel execution over email, phone, and LinkedIn. AiSDR sends deeply researched messages that can include multimedia content like videos, memes and voice notes.
### What is AiSDR used for?
AiSDR is used to automate outbound prospecting and meeting booking. It helps teams identify in-market prospects, write deeply researched messages, run multichannel sequences, and sync activity back to your CRM so reps can focus on qualified conversations instead of manual research and follow-ups.
### How much does AiSDR cost?
AiSDR offers monthly plans with no long-term lock-in. Plans include unlimited seats, all features unlocked, and 24/7 support. Pricing is based on message and lead search credits, with flexibility to cancel anytime.
### Does AiSDR integrate with my CRM?
Yes, AiSDR offers integrations with HubSpot and Salesforce. It syncs contact lists, enriches contact data, scores leads, and logs all outreach activity automatically.
### Can AiSDR handle inbound leads as well as outbound?
Yes, AiSDR works as a full AI sales assistant for inbound and outbound sales. It automatically follows up with inbound leads, qualifies prospects, answers questions, handles objections, and escalates complex situations to human salespeople.
### Is AI SDR better than a manual SDR?
AI SDRs are better for speed and consistent follow-ups, especially when you need to research leads and create messages based on your findings. Manual SDRs are better for high-context relationship-building, nuanced objection handling, and complex enterprise deals. Most teams get the best results by using an AI SDR to generate and qualify conversations, then having humans step in to close and expand accounts.
### What are good numbers for an SDR?
Metrics vary by industry, but common benchmarks include 40-50 calls per day, 10-40 emails per day, and booking at least 10-15 qualified meetings per month. A fully-ramped SDR should start hitting 5x their salary within 3-4 months to be cost-effective.
### How many leads can an SDR work in a day?
Depending on lead quality and process efficiency, an SDR can typically work ~15 leads per day, including calls, emails, and follow-ups.
### What is the average SDR to AE ratio?
A common ratio is 2-3 SDRs per AE, but this can vary depending on the complexity of the sales process, deal size, and sales cycle length.
### What does SDR stand for in sales?
SDR stands for Sales Development Representative. This is a sales role focused on generating and qualifying leads before passing them on to Account Executives (AEs) for closing deals.
### What does an SDR do?
An SDR researches potential customers, conducts cold outreach, qualifies leads, schedules meetings, and nurtures relationships in the early stages of the sales funnel.
